About the Project

Our CanSat Mission

Mission Overview

StratoBusters is an ambitious CanSat project designed to explore the stratosphere and collect valuable atmospheric data. Our mission combines cutting-edge technology with scientific research to advance our understanding of Earth's upper atmosphere.

Objectives

  • Measure atmospheric pressure and temperature at various altitudes
  • Collect environmental data during descent
  • Test and validate custom sensor systems
  • Demonstrate reliable data transmission and recovery
  • Contribute to atmospheric research

Technology

Our CanSat is equipped with state-of-the-art sensors, communication systems, and data logging capabilities. We've designed a robust system that can withstand the challenging conditions of the stratosphere while maintaining reliable operation.

Impact

The data collected by StratoBusters will contribute to atmospheric research and help validate new technologies for future space missions. This project represents a significant step forward in accessible space technology.
